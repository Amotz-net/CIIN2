What a Quality Passport certifies

A tonne of sargassum on a beach is a liability. The same tonne, with a verified record behind it, is a feedstock someone will buy, a mission a government can fund, and a claim a financier can underwrite. Three separate questions have to be answered before that is true, and answering them repeatably is what measurement, reporting and verification actually means in practice.

Traceability
Where the biomass came from and where it went. An unbroken custody chain from beach to gate, reconciled by mass balance — the method follows ISO 22095, the reconciliation tolerances are CIIN's own and published as such. An open manifest fails, whatever the numbers say.
Grade
How good it is, and what it may lawfully be used for. Deterministic rules score each batch on arsenic, foreign matter and hours since collection, then check the result against the governing standard for each destination channel — feed, fertiliser, compost, anaerobic digestion, controlled disposal. The channel's own limit decides, not one global grade.
Quality Passport
Whether the first two can be trusted. The certified document binding the grade to the referenced laboratory result and the custody record, each field carrying its confidence tier and the standard it cites.

The passport never grades itself. The laboratory measures, the agent ingests, the rules decide — and the rules are configuration, published and versioned, not code that has to be rebuilt to change.

How a recovery is coordinated

Landed sargassum degrades fast. Within roughly two days it is releasing hydrogen sulphide, smothering the seagrass underneath it, and has lost most of the value it arrived with. Coordination inside that window is the whole problem — and it spans actors who have never had a shared picture.

  1. Inundation is forecast for a stretch of coast

    Expected mass, arrival time and the beaches affected, before anything lands.

  2. Every role's own risk score moves

    A hotel sees beach access and guest health. A ministry sees public health, coastal health and carbon exposure. A recovery hub sees capacity load. One event, scored in each role's own terms, from the same underlying inputs — so the numbers never disagree with each other.

  3. The agent proposes a recovery plan

    No single hub can usually cover a surge alone. The agent pools hubs by distance, spare capacity and whether they can arrive inside the window, and proposes a combined mission with each hub's tonnage share. Where the pool still falls short, the shortfall is shown rather than hidden.

  4. A person approves it

    The agent proposes; it does not dispatch. Every recovery plan passes a human gate before anyone is mobilised, and the coastal asset grants access to the plan it can see.

  5. Pooled hubs run as one mission

    Collection is recorded against the mission — source beach, time, mass, custody — because a grade with no provenance certifies nothing.

  6. Evidence closes the loop

    A sample goes to an accredited laboratory. The result returns, the rules re-grade the batch on the confirmed number, permitted uses are set, and the passport issues. A buyer, a financier or a member of the public can then verify it.

What the agent decides, and what it is never allowed to decide

Coordination is a judgement problem: which hubs, in what combination, in what order, against a clock that is already running. Certification is not a judgement problem. CIIN separates the two deliberately, and the separation is what makes the output usable as evidence rather than as opinion.

What it reads
Inundation forecasts, hub location and spare capacity, mission history, access decisions and returned laboratory results — each scoped to the organisation entitled to it. The agent inherits the same row-level isolation every user has; it cannot see across a boundary a person could not.
What it proposes
Risk scores for each role in that role's own terms, hub combinations that can cover a load inside the window, mission plans with each hub's tonnage share, and the reasoning behind each — including the shortfall when the available capacity does not reach the tonnage on the beach.
What it never does
Dispatch a crew, set a grade, or issue a certificate. Rules grade, against published thresholds. People dispatch, on the record. Every proposal passes a human gate, and the gate is recorded as part of the evidence.

An agent permitted to grade would be an agent that could be wrong about a certificate. Judgement where judgement genuinely helps; deterministic rules wherever a claim depends on the answer.

What is running, and what is not

Most platforms in this space demonstrate a satellite feed they do not have. CIIN publishes the boundary instead — the same boundary the product shows on screen. Five states, and nothing is described as further along than it is.

  • Live

    Multi-tenant platform

    Accounts, invitation-only onboarding, organisations, and row-level isolation so an organisation sees only its own records. Deployed. Everything below sits on this spine, because retrofitting isolation afterwards is how verification systems fail.

  • Reviewed

    Grading and closure ruleset

    Thresholds reviewed against EU 2002/32/EC, EN 16202:2026 and ISO 22095-2:2026, with the reconciliation tolerances named as CIIN's own rather than borrowed authority. Held as configuration with a citation and a status against every value.

  • Demonstrated

    Coordination logic

    Risk scoring, alert routing, hub matching and capacity pooling, shown end to end — on representative data, labelled as representative wherever it appears. The logic is real; the coastline it runs on is not yet.

  • Sequenced

    Agentic coordination on live records

    The agent is built after the operational data layer and the first live feed, not before. An agent narrating over fixtures would contradict everything else on this page.

  • Integration point

    External feeds

    Satellite sargassum detection — research imagery under restricted licence, requiring permission and attribution, which is a partnership step rather than an engineering one. Also: messaging delivery for critical alerts, live occupancy data, and a dated, attributable carbon price source.

Verified

Matches a sourced standard limit or a laboratory result on record.

Directional

A defensible estimate with its assumptions named. A planning aid, never presented as an audited figure.

Representative

Illustrative data, marked as illustrative everywhere it appears.

Every figure on the platform carries one of these three tiers. Nothing representative is ever shown as though it were live.

A regional network, sovereign by default

A mass that leaves Jamaican waters becomes someone else's problem within days, but coastal data is sovereign and no country will surrender it to a shared system. So sharing is layered by what is genuinely safe to share, each layer an independent decision, and every cross-border figure carries the layer it was shared under.

  • Layer oneRegional early warning

    Detected mass, trajectory and arrival time for the next coast. A pure public good, and the safest layer to build first — days of lead time for a country that would otherwise be surprised.

  • Layer twoAnonymised benchmarking

    Aggregated performance across the network, with no country identifiable. Everyone learns; nobody exposes a weakness.

  • Layer threeOpt-in cooperation

    Cross-border resource exchange, hub mutual aid near shared maritime boundaries, and a shared picture for a mass hitting two coasts — scoped to that event and dissolved after it.

  • Layer fourComparable standards

    One certification framework, so a passport issued in Jamaica means the same to a buyer or a lender in the Dominican Republic. Without it, cross-border trade and carbon claims are noise.

CIIN provides the rails. It does not compel cooperation, and it holds no country's data by default — which is the only basis on which a neutral operator gets to exist.

Who works inside it

Access is by invitation and organised around organisations — each account inherits its role from the body it belongs to, and sees only that organisation's data. Certificate verification is the exception: it is open to everyone.

  • GovernmentPublic health, coastal and carbon risk; approves dispatch; the national coast view
  • Hotel or coastal assetIncoming sargassum to its frontage, access decisions, the clock
  • Recovery hubMission queue, collection, capacity pooling with other hubs
  • ProcessorBatch evidence, quality passport, certification, usage limits
  • Universities and laboratoriesSample testing, result validation, research data
  • BuyerVerified biomass, matched to what a channel actually permits
  • FinanceUnderwriting against verified operational performance
  • PublicVerify any certificate — no account, no login

Agentic coordination produces verified operational records. Verified records are what measurement, reporting and verification requires. MRV is what makes a coastal or carbon claim financeable. And one comparable standard across several coasts is what turns a national tool into regional public infrastructure.

CIIN is a verifier. It does not collect the biomass, broker the sale, or sell the carbon — it certifies what happened, and publishes how it knows.
